================================================================================
FIVE THEORETICAL GAMES  --  based on the rules implemented in Dama.java
================================================================================

These are illustrative, fully-legal games (every ply checked against the code's
rules) covering the three ways a game can be decided by this program -- a
resignation, a draw, and a side being left with no legal move:

    Game 1  -  Player 1 (RED)   wins   (by resignation)
    Game 2  -  Player 2 (BLACK) wins   (by resignation)
    Game 3  -  Draw
    Game 4  -  Player 1 (RED)   wins   (by capturing every enemy piece)
    Game 5  -  Player 2 (BLACK) wins   (by capturing every enemy piece)

Two constructed positions at the end also isolate the deferred-promotion rule.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BOARD, PLAYERS, AND NOTATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Board is 8x8, addressed as (row, col), row 0 at the TOP. Only the dark squares
where (row % 2 == col % 2) are playable (see DamaData.setUpGame).

  * Player 1 = RED  (r). Starts on rows 5-7. Moves UP the board (row decreases).
  * Player 2 = BLACK(b). Starts on rows 0-2. Moves DOWN the board (row increases).
  * RED moves first (doNewGame sets currentPlayer = RED).

Initial position:

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r0 |  b  .  b  .  b  .  b  . |   <- BLACK (Player 2) home rank (row 0)
 r1 |  .  b  .  b  .  b  .  b |
 r2 |  b  .  b  .  b  .  b  . |
 r3 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  - |     -  = empty playable square
 r4 |  -  .  -  .  -  .  -  . |     .  = non-playable (light) square
 r5 |  .  r  .  r  .  r  .  r |
 r6 |  r  .  r  .  r  .  r  . |
 r7 |  .  r  .  r  .  r  .  r |   <- RED (Player 1) home rank (row 7)

Move notation:
    (5,5)->(4,4)                 a simple (non-capturing) move
    (4,4)x(3,3)->(2,2)           a jump; the piece at the midpoint (3,3) is captured
    ...x(3,1)->(4,0)             continuation of a multi-jump (same turn)
    =K                           the piece is crowned a King on this square

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULES IN PLAY (as coded)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  This program implements FILIPINO DAMA (not American checkers):

  * Man movement: an ordinary man moves one square diagonally FORWARD only
    (RED up, BLACK down).  See immediateMoves.
  * Man capture: a man captures an adjacent enemy in ANY diagonal direction --
    forward OR backward -- landing on the empty square just beyond it.  Backward
    capture is a real Dama rule, not a checkers rule.  See immediateCaptures. See A.1.
  * Flying kings: once crowned, a king slides any number of empty squares along a
    diagonal, and captures by flying over the first enemy on a diagonal (only empty
    squares in between) to land on ANY empty square beyond it.  See immediateMoves /
    immediateCaptures (king branch).
  * Mandatory capture: if any capture is available, the side to move MUST capture
    (getLegalMoves returns only captures when captures exist).
  * MAXIMUM capture: among the available captures you MUST play a sequence that
    captures the greatest number of pieces; only the first steps of a longest
    sequence are offered (getLegalMoves / getLegalJumpsFrom rank chains by length
    via maxCaptures).
  * Multi-jump: after a capture, if the same piece can capture again it MUST
    continue, all in one turn (doMakeMove loops via getLegalJumpsFrom).
  * Deferred promotion (A.2): reaching the back row crowns the piece AND ends the
    turn -- UNLESS that landing was a jump and a further capture is available from
    that square AS A MAN, in which case the piece stays a man and keeps jumping,
    only crowning if it finally STOPS on the back row (makeMove).  Promotion ends
    the turn: a man crowned by a jump does NOT continue capturing as a king in the
    same move (doMakeMove's justPromoted guard).
  * A game ends when the player to move has no legal move (getLegalMoves == null,
    a loss), OR by the Resign button, OR by the Draw button.

  (The examples below use only men, never kings, so they do not exercise flying-king
  moves.  Games 1-3 use single forced captures; Games 4-5 show a forced maximum-capture
  chain, but none present a CHOICE between capture sequences of different lengths.
  Every ply is still legal under the rules above.)

SCORING (the corrected point system)
  * +1 to the mover for each completed turn (a simple move, a single capture, or a
    whole multi-jump chain -- a chain still counts as one move / +1).
  * +5 to the mover on the turn a piece is crowned King.
  * +10 to the OPPONENT when a player resigns (doResign).
  * A draw awards no bonus (gameDraw).
  * P1moves / P2moves count one per completed turn for that player.

================================================================================
GAME 1  --  PLAYER 1 (RED) WINS   (by a winning multi-jump, then BLACK resigns)
================================================================================

Ply  Player  Move                              Notes
---  ------  --------------------------------  -----------------------------------
 1   RED     (5,5)->(4,4)                       develop; no jump available
 2   BLACK   (2,0)->(3,1)                       develop
 3   RED     (6,6)->(5,5)                       waiting move; still no jump forced
 4   BLACK   (2,2)->(3,3)                       vacates (2,2) -- a blunder: it now
                                                 gives RED a forced double-jump
 5   RED     (4,4)x(3,3)->(2,2)x(3,1)->(4,0)    FORCED multi-jump, TWO captures.
                                                 2nd leg (2,2)->(4,0) is a BACKWARD
                                                 capture (allowed, A.1). Whole chain
                                                 = one move = +1. BLACK now down 2.
 6   BLACK   resigns                            It is BLACK's turn; BLACK clicks
                                                 Resign -> Player 1 (RED) wins,
                                                 Player 1 gets +10 (doResign).

Position just before BLACK resigns (RED = 12 men, BLACK = 10 men):

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r0 |  b  .  b  .  b  .  b  . |
 r1 |  .  b  .  b  .  b  .  b |
 r2 |  -  .  -  .  b  .  b  . |
 r3 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  - |
 r4 |  r  .  -  .  -  .  -  . |   (RED man that just landed on (4,0))
 r5 |  .  r  .  r  .  r  .  r |
 r6 |  r  .  r  .  r  .  -  . |
 r7 |  .  r  .  r  .  r  .  r |

Running score:
    turn 1 RED   +1   -> P1 = 1   (P1moves 1)
    turn 2 BLACK +1   -> P2 = 1   (P2moves 1)
    turn 3 RED   +1   -> P1 = 2   (P1moves 2)
    turn 4 BLACK +1   -> P2 = 2   (P2moves 2)
    turn 5 RED   +1   -> P1 = 3   (P1moves 3)   (multi-jump chain = a single +1)
    resign       +10  -> P1 = 13
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    FINAL:  P1 = 13 points, 3 moves    P2 = 2 points, 2 moves    RED WINS

Stats written (player1win): both gamesplayed +1; RED gameswin +1, totalpoints +13,
highestpoint = 13 (vs BLACK), fewestmoves = 3 (first win record); BLACK gameslose +1,
totalpoints +2, highestpoint = 2.

GAME 2  --  PLAYER 2 (BLACK) WINS   (a winning backward multi-jump, then RED resigns)
================================================================================

Ply  Player  Move                              Notes
---  ------  --------------------------------  -----------------------------------
 1   RED     (5,5)->(4,4)                       develop; no jump available
 2   BLACK   (2,4)->(3,3)                        develop. Note it comes from (2,4),
                                                 leaving (2,2) occupied, so RED's man
                                                 on (4,4) CANNOT jump (3,3) (landing
                                                 (2,2) is blocked) -- RED is not forced.
 3   RED     (5,7)->(4,6)                        a blunder: RED now has men on (4,4)
                                                 and (4,6) with (5,5)/(5,7) empty behind
 4   BLACK   (3,3)x(4,4)->(5,5)x(4,6)->(3,7)     FORCED multi-jump, TWO captures.
                                                 2nd leg (5,5)->(3,7) is a BACKWARD
                                                 capture (BLACK moving up; allowed, A.1).
                                                 Whole chain = one move = +1. RED down 2.
 5   RED     resigns                            It is RED's turn; down two men, RED
                                                 clicks Resign -> Player 2 (BLACK) wins,
                                                 Player 2 gets +10 (doResign).

Position just before RED resigns (RED = 10 men, BLACK = 12 men):

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r0 |  b  .  b  .  b  .  b  . |
 r1 |  .  b  .  b  .  b  .  b |
 r2 |  b  .  b  .  -  .  b  . |   (2,4) is empty -- that man is the one now on (3,7)
 r3 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  b |   (BLACK man that ended the jump on (3,7))
 r4 |  -  .  -  .  -  .  -  . |
 r5 |  .  r  .  r  .  -  .  - |   (5,5) and (5,7) empty
 r6 |  r  .  r  .  r  .  r  . |
 r7 |  .  r  .  r  .  r  .  r |

Running score:
    turn 1 RED   +1   -> P1 = 1   (P1moves 1)
    turn 2 BLACK +1   -> P2 = 1   (P2moves 1)
    turn 3 RED   +1   -> P1 = 2   (P1moves 2)
    turn 4 BLACK +1   -> P2 = 2   (P2moves 2)   (multi-jump chain = a single +1)
    resign       +10  -> P2 = 12
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    FINAL:  P2 = 12 points, 2 moves    P1 = 2 points, 2 moves    BLACK WINS

Stats written (player2win): both gamesplayed +1; BLACK gameswin +1, totalpoints +12,
highestpoint = 12 (vs RED), fewestmoves = 2 (first win record); RED gameslose +1,
totalpoints +2, highestpoint = 2.

================================================================================
GAME 3  --  DRAW   (an even exchange, then the players agree to a draw)
================================================================================

Ply  Player  Move                              Notes
---  ------  --------------------------------  -----------------------------------
 1   RED     (5,3)->(4,2)                       develop
 2   BLACK   (2,2)->(3,3)                        develop
 3   RED     (5,5)->(4,4)                        develop; no jump forced yet
 4   BLACK   (3,3)x(4,4)->(5,5)                  FORCED: BLACK must take the man on
                                                 (4,4) (mandatory capture). +1.
 5   RED     (6,6)x(5,5)->(4,4)                  FORCED recapture. Material is even
                                                 again (11 men each). +1.
 6   BLACK   offers Draw                         Position is balanced; BLACK clicks
                                                 Draw -> the game ends in a draw
                                                 (gameDraw). No point bonus.

Position at the draw agreement (RED = 11 men, BLACK = 11 men):

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r0 |  b  .  b  .  b  .  b  . |
 r1 |  .  b  .  b  .  b  .  b |
 r2 |  b  .  -  .  b  .  b  . |
 r3 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  - |
 r4 |  -  .  r  .  r  .  -  . |   (RED men on (4,2) and (4,4))
 r5 |  .  r  .  -  .  -  .  r |
 r6 |  r  .  r  .  r  .  -  . |
 r7 |  .  r  .  r  .  r  .  r |

Running score:
    turn 1 RED   +1   -> P1 = 1   (P1moves 1)
    turn 2 BLACK +1   -> P2 = 1   (P2moves 1)
    turn 3 RED   +1   -> P1 = 2   (P1moves 2)
    turn 4 BLACK +1   -> P2 = 2   (P2moves 2)   (a capture is still just one move)
    turn 5 RED   +1   -> P1 = 3   (P1moves 3)   (recapture, one move)
    draw         +0
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    FINAL:  P1 = 3 points, 3 moves    P2 = 2 points, 2 moves    DRAW

Stats written (gameDraw): both gamesplayed +1 and gamesdraw +1; RED totalpoints +3,
highestpoint = 3; BLACK totalpoints +2, highestpoint = 2. A draw is NOT a win, so it
sets no "fewest moves to win" record for either player (C.9).

================================================================================
GAME 4  --  PLAYER 1 (RED) WINS   (by capturing ALL of Player 2's pieces)
================================================================================

Games 1-3 ended by resignation or a draw; Games 4 and 5 show the third outcome
the code detects on its own: a side that loses its LAST piece has no legal move,
so getLegalMoves returns null and gameOver declares the other player the winner.

These two are CONSTRUCTED positions (like the promotion examples below), not played
from the opening, and the moves are not meant to be strategically sensible -- they
just illustrate a legal win by total capture.  Every ply was verified against the
engine (mandatory + maximum capture forces the whole chain).

Starting position (all other squares empty).  RED to move:

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r0 |  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  . |
 r1 |  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  . |
 r2 |  -  .  r  .  -  .  -  . |   RED man on (2,2)
 r3 |  .  b  .  b  .  -  .  - |   BLACK men on (3,1) and (3,3)
 r4 |  -  .  -  .  -  .  -  . |
 r5 |  .  b  .  b  .  -  .  - |   BLACK men on (5,1) and (5,3)
 r6 |  -  .  -  .  -  .  -  . |
 r7 |  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  . |

Ply  Player  Move                                          Notes
---  ------  ----------------------------------------      ---------------------
 1   RED     (2,2)x(3,3)->(4,4)x(5,3)->(6,2)               ONE forced maximum
             x(5,1)->(4,0)x(3,1)->(2,2)                    capture, FOUR pieces.
                                                            Legs 1 and 2 are
                                                            BACKWARD captures
                                                            (allowed, A.1).  The
                                                            man loops back to
                                                            (2,2); not the back
                                                            row, so no King.
                                                            Whole chain = +1.

After the chain BLACK has 0 pieces.  It becomes BLACK's turn, getLegalMoves(BLACK)
== null, so: "Player 2 has no moves.  Player 1 wins." (player1win).

Final position (RED = 1 man on (2,2), BLACK = 0):

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r0 |  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  . |
 r1 |  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  . |
 r2 |  -  .  r  .  -  .  -  . |   the lone RED man, back where it started
 r3 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  - |
 r4 |  -  .  -  .  -  .  -  . |
 r5 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  - |
 r6 |  -  .  -  .  -  .  -  . |
 r7 |  .  .  .  .  .  .  .  . |

Running score:
    turn 1 RED   +1   -> P1 = 1   (P1moves 1)   (a 4-capture chain is one move)
    ---------------------------------------------------------------------
    FINAL:  P1 = 1 point, 1 move    P2 = 0 points, 0 moves    RED WINS

Stats written (player1win): both gamesplayed +1; RED gameswin +1, totalpoints +1,
highestpoint = 1 (vs BLACK), fewestmoves = 1 (first win record); BLACK gameslose +1.

================================================================================
PROMOTION EXAMPLES (rule A.2)  --  two constructed positions, not from the opening
================================================================================

The interlocked starting formation makes a King impossible to earn this early, so
these are small set-up positions (all other squares empty) that isolate the rule.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Example A  --  a jump that STOPS on the back row: the piece crowns, turn ends.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r5 |  .  -  .  -  .  b  .  - |   BLACK man on (5,5)
 r6 |  -  .  -  .  -  .  r  . |   RED man on (6,6)
 r7 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  - |   (7,7) empty     (all other squares empty)

BLACK to move:  (5,5)x(6,6)->(7,7)=K

At (7,7) there is no further capture, so the man is crowned a King and the turn
ends. Score to BLACK: +1 (the move) and +5 (the King) = +6.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Example B  --  the exception: a jump PASSES OVER the back row and keeps capturing,
               so promotion is SKIPPED and the piece stays a man.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      c0 c1 c2 c3 c4 c5 c6 c7
 r5 |  .  -  .  -  .  b  .  - |   BLACK man on (5,5)
 r6 |  -  .  r  .  r  .  -  . |   RED men on (6,2) and (6,4)
 r7 |  .  -  .  -  .  -  .  - |   (7,3) empty, (5,1) empty   (all other squares empty)

BLACK to move:  (5,5)x(6,4)->(7,3) ... x(6,2)->(5,1)

The first leg lands on (7,3) -- the back row -- but a further capture is available
from there, so (per A.2) the piece does NOT crown; it stays a man and MUST keep
jumping: (7,3)x(6,2)->(5,1). It finishes on (5,1), which is not the back row, so it
remains an ORDINARY MAN (no King). Score to BLACK: +1 for the move (a multi-jump
chain is still one move), and NO King bonus.
